Atlas

Atlas computes with polynomial rings over the rationals and resolves hypersurface singularities. You can run a calculation at the prompt, follow a resolution chart by chart, or look up a function in the manual.

Work with Atlas¶

The first calculation downloads a compiled copy of Atlas. It runs on your computer, and your polynomials stay in this browser tab. Download any resolution records you want to keep. You can also run the tools offline.

Resolve a plane cusp¶

Which exceptional divisor does the branch of the cusp y2=x3 meet? In the terminal, name the coordinates and ask the resolution for the branch's attachment.

atlas> x, y = coordinates("x", "y")
atlas> K = resolve(y^2 - x^3)
atlas> K.branches[0].meets
[E3]

The branch meets E3, the exceptional curve from the third blowup. Follow the branch through the charts to see where they intersect.
