Atlas browser runtime

The Atlas native programs are built from the source revision in REVISION.
The compressed initramfs contains Ubuntu 26.10 AMD64 CPython, BusyBox and
the libraries loaded by Atlas. Package copyright notices are inside the
guest under /usr/share/doc and common licenses under /usr/share/common-licenses.
Ubuntu source packages are available at https://launchpad.net/ubuntu/stonking.

The emulator, Linux kernel and PC firmware are redistributed from Kohei
Tokunaga's QEMU Wasm demo images, commit b7c549b5e6f4c376f76483a03e983214421434ad.
Their upstream files and build instructions are available here:
https://github.com/ktock/qemu-wasm-demo-images/tree/b7c549b5e6f4c376f76483a03e983214421434ad/alpine-x86_64
https://github.com/ktock/qemu-wasm-demo/blob/main/create-images.sh
https://github.com/ktock/qemu-wasm
